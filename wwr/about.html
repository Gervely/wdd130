About Rafting Compass

Rafting Compass is a passionate company that guides adventurers through the most spectacular rapids. During adventure season, our experienced team invites everyone to discover the power of the river and the beauty of untamed landscapes. Whether it's a family outing or an extreme challenge, we provide safe, memorable, and adrenaline‑filled experiences. With Rafting Compass, every descent becomes a true exploration where nature and teamwork come together.

History

Founded by a group of river enthusiasts more than a decade ago, Rafting Compass began as a small local initiative to share the thrill of white water rafting with friends and families. Over the years, the company grew steadily, guided by a passion for adventure and a commitment to safety. From its humble beginnings on regional rivers, Rafting Compass expanded its offerings to include diverse trips for beginners and experts alike. Today, it stands as a trusted name in rafting, combining professional expertise with a spirit of exploration that continues to inspire adventurers from around the world.
